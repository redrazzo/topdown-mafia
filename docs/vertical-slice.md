# Vertical Slice Checklist

## Gameplay loop

- Walk to the assignment point.
- Enter the designated sedan.
- Drive to the target building.
- Exit the car cleanly.
- Trigger the interior transition.
- Complete the back-office handoff.
- Advance the mission state without a soft-lock.

## Content targets

- One compact exterior district.
- One drivable period car.
- One enterable back-office interior.
- One visible objective HUD.
- One opening chapter director coordinating beats.

## Polishing priorities

- Car handling readability from a top-down camera.
- Strong arrival framing on the destination building.
- Fade timing that feels deliberate rather than gamey.
- Prompt text that reads as grounded crime-drama instruction, not arcade clutter.

## Explicitly out of scope

- combat
- police response
- wider traffic simulation
- economy
- multiple crime families
- open-ended sandbox systems
